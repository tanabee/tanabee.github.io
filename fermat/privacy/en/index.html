Privacy Policy

App: FERMAT
Developer: tanabee
Last updated: 2026-11-15

FERMAT ("the app") is a memory game for Android in which you memorize where the numbers are and tap them in ascending order. This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and with whom it is shared.

プライバシーポリシー（日本語）

1. Information the app itself collects

The app does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, contacts, or precise location. The app has no server of its own and does not send your play records anywhere. No account is required.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to a server by the app.

  • The highest stage you have cleared
  • Whether you have finished the practice stage
  • Images of the screen at the moment you clear a stage (for sharing; the latest one and the one for your best record)

All of this is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Sharing

When you tap "Share" or the share button on the stage list, your device's share sheet opens. The message and the image are passed only to the app you choose. The app never sends them anywhere unless you tap the button.

4. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party service. It collects and processes information independently of the app, under its own privacy policy.

4-1. Google AdMob

The app may show full-screen ads served by Google AdMob when you retry a stage. AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as:

  • Advertising identifier (Android advertising ID)
  • IP address and the approximate location inferred from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, etc.)
  • Interactions with ads, such as impressions and taps
  • Diagnostic information about ad delivery (such as whether an ad loaded and how long it took)

This information is used for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, frequency capping, and fraud prevention. For details, see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy

4-2. Managing your advertising ID

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads

5. Data retention

Because the app has no server of its own, the app itself does not retain any data. The third-party service retains data according to the policies linked above.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not primarily directed at children under 13. The app never asks users to enter personal information such as a name or email address, and does not collect personal information on its own. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us at the address below and we will respond appropriately.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as needed. Significant changes will be reflected on this page together with the "Last updated" date above.
